        # Docs update for #202

        Run two-sided demand validation for WorldGraph

        ## Requested

        ## Summary

Validate that WorldGraph solves a recurring problem on both sides of the proposed registry before approving an implementation roadmap. Desk research demonstrates market activity and fragmentation; it does not prove that creators will claim profiles or that discovery users will return.

## Hypotheses

### Supply

Creators and small studios will provide a project URL, review a generated manifest, correct it, and permit publication because a canonical verified profile improves legibility, distribution, collaboration, or inbound opportunities.

### Demand

Developers, producers, innovation teams, and IP/rightsholders will use structured search and profiles to find and evaluate worlds more efficiently than platform-by-platform browsing or unstructured web search.

### Monetization

Basic listing should remain free. At least one segment may value verified management, analytics, private scouting, API access, qualified leads, or rights/licensing workflow enough to pay later.

## Research plan

### Interviews

Complete at minimum:

- 8 creator/studio interviews across at least three World categories
- 6 discovery-side interviews across developers/producers, innovation teams, or IP/rightsholders

Use problem interviews before presenting the solution. Record current workflows, frequency, time/cost, existing alternatives, trust requirements, and the last real example of the problem.

### Concierge test

- Create private profiles for at least 10 consenting creator projects.
- Ask each creator to review and correct the manifest.
- Measure time, disputed fields, missing fields, and willingness to claim/publish.
- Do not publish without explicit approval.

### Discovery test

Give at least six participants realistic search tasks using a prototype or curated corpus. Examples:

- find an interactive narrative world supporting creator collaboration
- find a world with runtime AI agents and a web entry point
- compare two projects’ rights and integration information
- identify a project suitable for a licensing conversation

Track task completion, confidence, missing filters, outbound action, and stated repeat-use context.

### Willingness-to-pay test

Test concrete packages without charging:

- verified/managed profile
- private scouting workspace
- API/data access
- qualified inbound and analytics
- rights/licensing support

Do not ask “would you pay?” in the abstract. Ask participants to rank alternatives, name budget ownership, and describe the last comparable purchase.

## Decision gate

Recommend proceed, iterate, or stop. A proceed recommendation requires evidence of both:

- creators completing claim/review and approving publication
- discovery users completing real tasks and identifying a repeat-use context

Page views, compliments, waitlist emails, and generic interest are not sufficient on their own.

## Required deliverables

- docs/worldgraph/VALIDATION_PLAN.md
- interview scripts and recruitment criteria
- consent and research-data handling guidance
- anonymized interview notes
- concierge-test results
- discovery-task results
- monetization evidence
- docs/worldgraph/VALIDATION_READOUT.md with proceed/iterate/stop recommendation

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Minimum participant counts and segment coverage are met.
- [ ] Interviews use recent behavior rather than hypothetical enthusiasm.
- [ ] No private project is published without explicit creator consent.
- [ ] Creator correction time and disputed fields are measured.
- [ ] Discovery tasks and success criteria are defined before testing.
- [ ] Supply, demand, and monetization evidence are reported separately.
- [ ] Contradictory and negative evidence is preserved.
- [ ] Personally identifying research notes are not committed.
- [ ] The final recommendation states what evidence would change the decision.
- [ ] The MVP PRD is not approved until this gate is reviewed.

## Dependencies

Requires the research corpus and journey prototype. Human recruitment and interviews are an explicit external dependency; the issue may produce the plan first but must not claim validation before fieldwork is complete.
